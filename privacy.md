# Privacy Policy – IRemember: Private Personal Memory

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

IRemember: Private Personal Memory ("the App") helps users store and manage personal notes, passwords, voice recordings, photos, documents, and reminders.

The App is designed to store user-created memories locally on your device. We do not operate our own cloud servers to store or synchronize your memories.

## 2. Information and Permissions

The App may process the following information locally:

- Notes, passwords, tags, and personal instructions.
- Voice recordings and supported transcription text.
- Photos and imported documents.
- Reminder schedules and notification preferences.

The App may request camera, microphone, biometric, notification, and alarm permissions to provide its features. These permissions are used for their stated purposes when the relevant features are enabled.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following Google services:

**Google AdMob:** If advertisements are enabled, Google may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising and related purposes.

**Google Play Billing:** Google Play processes purchases. The App may receive purchase information to verify access to paid features. We do not intentionally collect your full payment card details.

**Google ML Kit:** The App may use text recognition to extract text from images. Processing depends on the configured ML Kit implementation.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Advertising Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Deletion

User-created memories are intended to remain in the App's private storage on your device. We use available device security features, including optional biometric authentication where supported, to help protect access.

You can delete memories using the App's available deletion features. If supported, you can also export your data as a backup.

Uninstalling the App normally removes its private local data, but exported files and device backups may remain. You are responsible for protecting exported copies.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you have concerns about children's information or privacy practices, contact the developer below.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information. You can manage locally stored memories through the App's available features. Requests concerning information processed by third-party services may need to be directed to those services.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the same public URL with a revised date.
